Scope: Caseshelf app shell — Shelf (one-case feed), Stacks, Capture sheet, Wishlist, case editor. Visitor mode: Operate.
Audience/job: one collector, phone in hand beside their shelf in the evening, adding and handling their own cases.
Constraints: local-only, IndexedDB, no real cover art; every 3D action has a button.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One case owns the whole screen and is handled like an object; refuses the cover-grid-plus-detail-page arrangement every collection app ships.

OWN-WORLD: Void #0a0a0a to slate #1e2026 ground lit by a soft lamp tinted from the current case's cover; fog #5a6472 and sand #c7b9a6 for secondary text and selection; pure white line glyphs in one 1.75 stroke; tall condensed display (Antonio) for titles, Inter for body; catalog numbers in tabular figures; case plastic colors (PS blue, Xbox green, Switch red, DVD black, Blu-ray blue) are the only saturation.

STORY: The collector sees their copy, turns it, opens it, pulls the disc, scores it, and flicks to the next; Stacks gives the whole shelf at true spine thickness when one-at-a-time is too slow.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Games/Movies tabs top centre; the 3D case centred at ~60% height; right rail Flip, Open, Disc, Score; catalog No., platform, year, title in display type and status low left over a bottom scrim; tab bar Shelf · Stacks · Capture · Wishlist.

FORM: challenger pop-culture-shelf-vertical-video-feed-surface (user-chosen over assigned candidate 3, Collection Store). Signature interaction: drag-to-turn case with momentum and spring-home, hinge-open, disc eject; ambient lamp recolours per case on snap. Raises kept: fixed tabular cells for numbers and A–Z index; true relative case scale; two type sizes per list. seed:75115d2828226749

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
